Captain Jack Sparrow's Last Haul

For forty years, every pirate crew alive has chased the same rumor: Captain Jack Sparrow's Last Haul — a treasure so vast he stopped raiding ships and started raiding banks. When the Navy cornered him, Sparrow didn't bury one map. He burned it, tore it into fragments, and hid each piece behind a mechanism meant to drown a navy scout and reward a true pirate.

You've reached the wreck of the Black Beacon.

Chart the course. Recover every fragment. Find the X. Claim the haul.

Captain's Log — Entry I

The Three Fragments

Sparrow's chart was no single sheet of parchment. Before his capture, he divided the map into three torn fragments — each protected by a different lock. The locks are not iron and key. They are observation, patience, and the pirate's oldest skill: looking where others don't think to look.

Collect all sub-flags hidden across the wreck. When you hold them, concatenate them in sequence — reassembling the map. Feed the assembled chart through the vault mechanism to calculate the master bearing pointing to the X.
